At Contactous, we design and build scalable applications, data platforms, and automated workflow engines powered by our proven AI methodologies and technical frameworks. Built specifically for technical teams, engineering leads, and enterprise architects, our solutions eliminate the friction of processing unstructured data, running complex ETL pipelines, and managing specialized enterprise data workflows. Whether deployed as hosted applications on your private cloud or integrated directly via APIs, every solution is architected for strict governance, custom schemas, and high operational throughput.

Our enterprise and data quality offerings give technical teams immediate access to intelligent data management and natural-language query tools without building from scratch:

* **Self-Service Business Analytics (NL2SQL):** A secure natural-language data layer that converts text to structured SQL queries across your business domain, deployed directly onto your private cloud with full governance.

* **Data Quality & Entity Resolution:** High-performance deduplication and fuzzy-matching engine proven across tens of millions of records for database purging, data preparation, and record linking.

* **Document Data Extraction:** Configurable extraction engine that parses unstructured documents and images into clean key-value pairs, integrating seamlessly into OCR, RPA, and ETL pipelines.

* **Lightweight Pre-CRM & Field Operations:** Flexible, Kanban-style Deal Tracker for external B2B partner pipelines, alongside customizable mobile/web Contact Management platforms for field agent logging and document distribution.

For education technology and learning platforms, our specialized AI engines turn raw content and outcome taxonomies into production-grade courseware and dynamic learning tools:

* **AI Content Generation:** Powered by *ContentWeaver* for structured LMS course mapping and *SandStorm* (built on Stanford's open-source STORM engine) for multi-perspective research and topic synthesis.

* **AI Objective Assessment Engine:** API-driven question generation (MCQs, FIBs, SAQs, Cloze) mapped directly to Bloom's, SOLO, and Webb's Depth of Knowledge learning frameworks.

* **Adaptive Quizzing (HotKettle):** A curriculum-agnostic Automated Query Generator (AQG) built for mastery-based learning, complete with real-time feedback and dynamic difficulty switches.

* **Custom Engineering Services:** Bespoke engineering across custom LLM applications, unstructured data ingestion, vector search implementations, and custom ETL pipelines tailored to your domain stack.
