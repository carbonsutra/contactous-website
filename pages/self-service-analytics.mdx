We empower business teams get answers from data in minutes instead of days - without writing SQL or waiting on analysts. We achieve this by designing a secure natural-language data access layer for our customer's specific business domains. Our NL2SQL methodology for democratized data access covers governance, semantic modeling, and security, and results in an application hosted on customer's private cloud. 

## Self-Service Business Analytics​ 
We design a natural-language data access layer for your business domains. It is a custom service that results in a usable application within a private cloud, on-premise or our hosted servers, optionally detached from any live database. 

Our customers are able to communicate with their proprietary and legacy databases, with security, auditability and business context. 

## Our Approach

1. We start with discussing the requirements with your stakeholders, studying the data and then submit a plan with clear deliverables, outcomes and ROI calculation.

2. Our recommended approach is to test the application with a proof-of-concept (POC), within a specific domain (or part of database). 

3. Once the POC is successful, we deliver the application based on the plan and agreed timeline, hosted on a private cloud.
