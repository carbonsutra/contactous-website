##  Unlock the Hidden Value of Your Digital Assets

**Secure, Deep Metadata Extraction for the Publishing Industry**

To thrive in the modern publishing landscape, content discovery is everything. Metadata about an asset, like a book, is of very high importance in building systems, driving e-commerce, and powering content browsing sites.

Scholia is a premium data-extraction service tailored explicitly for publishers and all players in the industry who deal in books. We transform static documents into rich, structured data—giving your systems the exact fuel they need to connect your publications with the readers, researchers, and institutions searching for them.

**The Power of Standardized, Deep Metadata**
Basic titles and author names are no longer enough to compete. We extract deep, relational, and derived metadata from your documents to help you categorize knowledge into globally recognized taxonomies. We map your content to standardized library classification systems like the Library of Congress Classification (LCC) and the Dewey Decimal System (DDC), as well as commercial trade systems like BISAC and Thema.

**What Scholia Extracts?**
Large Language Models excel at distilling both explicitly stated and implicitly derived metadata from dense academic texts. Whether you are handling books, textbooks, research reports, or scholarly articles published in an academic journal, Scholia provides unparalleled data synthesis.

For **Books and Textbooks**, our extraction engine captures:

- Standard front matter, allowing us to extract the title, publisher, publication year, and ISBN.
- A comprehensive, detailed 3-paragraph summary of the book content.
- A concise, single-line summary or tagline of the book.
- A specialized list of at least 10 unique keywords specific to the book's subject matter.
- Relevant categories or subject classifications, such as Computers, Biology, or Management.
- A set of exactly 10 medium-to-hard level conceptual or problem-solving questions that the book addresses.

For **Academic Papers and Scholarly Articles**, our extraction deeply analyzes the standardized IMRAD format, including the introduction, methods, results, and discussion:

- We meticulously extract front matter like authors, affiliations, DOI, and venue metadata.
- We also summarize the core problem statement, key contributions, and methodology taxonomy.
- We map out granular content sections, paragraphs, and even assets such as figures, tables, and equations.
- We accurately capture raw text and citation keys from document references.
