Founded a decade ago in Singapore, Contactous started by building custom software, tailored AI applications, and enterprise data solutions for businesses, publishers, and educational institutions. Over the past ten years, our infrastructure has grown to support thousands of active users and process millions of requests annually across mission-critical data workflows. Built on battle-tested frameworks for natural-language data access (NL2SQL), entity resolution, and unstructured document extraction, our solutions deliver the operational clarity, security, and governance modern technical teams require.

Today, we put developer-first, domain-tuned APIs at the center of everything we build. From automated learning assessments and carbon footprint calculations to RAG-as-a-Service, our plug-and-play APIs come standard with native Model Context Protocol (MCP) server support, OpenAPI documentation, and enterprise compliance. Whether you are deploying our ready-made API portfolio or partnering with us to build bespoke, custom endpoints on your private cloud, Contactous provides the scalable foundation that lets engineering teams ship specialized AI capabilities rapidly and confidently.
