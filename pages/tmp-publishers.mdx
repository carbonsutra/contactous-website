# For Publishers

Research and content workflows designed for publishing, editorial, and scholarly teams.

## Example workflows

- **Discover:** identify the API or product that fits the workflow.
- **Prototype:** use sample requests and dummy data in the [Playground](/playground).
- **Integrate:** connect approved endpoints to your application and validate outputs.
- **Operationalize:** add monitoring, access controls, and review processes appropriate to your organization.

## Recommended starting points

- [Scholia](/apis/scholia)
- [ContentWeaver](/apis/contentweaver)
- [2Markdown](/apis/2markdown)
- [DeDuplication](/apis/deduplication)

## Sample scenario

A team sends a representative input, reviews the structured result, and then adapts the integration to its own data and operational requirements. The scenario and all sample values on this page are illustrative, not production guarantees.
