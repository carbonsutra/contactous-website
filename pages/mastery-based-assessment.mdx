HotKettle is an assessment tool to measure a student's understanding of a lesson through personalized challenge. Based on principles of Mastery-based Adaptive Learning, it is an AI-based Adaptive Testing application used by instructors to empower students to self-assess their knowledge.

Our users are instructors from Education (Higher Education, K-12) and trainers from Corporate (Learning & Development, Compliance & Certification Training, Onboarding & Skill Development) sectors.
